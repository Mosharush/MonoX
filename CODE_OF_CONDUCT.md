# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory behavior, doxxing and deliberate disruption are not accepted. Discuss the
technical decision without attacking the person. Respect privacy and never post another person's credentials,
customer data or private source.

Report conduct concerns privately to `moshe@haru.sh`. The maintainer may edit or remove content, pause a
discussion, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and useful.
